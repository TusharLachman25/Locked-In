🔒 Locked In — Beta V1.0 -> Beta V2.0 Update

🔔 Notifications + push
Get notified when someone likes/comments on your post, follows you, posts a workout, or messages you. Bell icon top-right of feed shows everything. Pushes hit your phone too. Toggle any of them on/off in profile settings.

👥 View each other's profiles
Tap anyone's name on the leaderboard, in a story, or in search to open their profile. Follow/unfollow from there. You can also edit your bio now (no more "sweat now shine later" for everyone).

📖 Stories work properly now
Hold to pause, release to resume. Viewed stories show a grey ring, unviewed stay colored. Unviewed stories appear first, like Instagram. Auto-advances to the next person when one user's stories finish.

❤️ Likes, comments, views
On stories AND on profile posts. You can see who viewed your stories (only you). Tap a post in someone's grid to see likes and leave comments.

💬 Chat improvements
"Seen" indicator under messages so you know when someone's read it. Tapping a chat notification jumps straight to the conversation.

🏏 3 new sports + better picker
Added Cricket, Volleyball, and Beach Volleyball. The activity picker is now a grid of icons with a search bar instead of swiping through 12 options.

⚖️ Points rebalanced
Gym and sports were getting destroyed by runners/swimmers. Now they actually compete. Quick numbers: running 13/km, swim 7/100m, comp football 1.1/min, badminton 0.95, padel 0.85, gym 0.75. Full breakdown on the (i) icon next to PTS on the leaderboard.

Plus a bunch of smaller fixes — captions look cleaner, signup actually saves your info, manual posts work on web now, leaderboard names don't get cut off.
